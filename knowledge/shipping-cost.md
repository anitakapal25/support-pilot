# Shipping costs

## Rates and free shipping

Standard shipping costs $5 for orders below $50 and is free for orders of $50 or more before tax. Express shipping costs $12 for every order. The checkout total shows the applicable shipping cost before payment.

## Rate table

| Service | Order value | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | Below $50 | $5 |
| Standard | $50 or more | $0 |
| Express | Any value | $12 |
